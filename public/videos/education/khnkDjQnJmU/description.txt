Have you ever seen an object spin like a top on a frozen surface without stopping? It looks like a magic trick, but it's actually a fascinating display of physics. When a high-velocity metal object lands on smooth ice at a precise angle, its residual heat melts a microscopic layer of water underneath. This creates a friction-less cushion, trapping the object in a state of near-perfect rotation. It can spin at thousands of RPMs for minutes at a time. It’s a rare intersection where extreme temperature meets perfect momentum, creating a visual that looks like a glitch in reality.
